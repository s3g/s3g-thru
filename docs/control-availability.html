Control reference

When controls are available

A small hollow square means that encoder position has no effect in the current mode or engine setting. The stored value remains available if you return to a mode that uses it. Some whole pages are omitted when their controls cannot apply.

Control or pageUnavailable whenReason
STAK, RATE and Stack PathQuad and SequenceEach part is fixed to one audio lane. Sequence retains its trigs while audio in that lane changes.
Cutups ADV, lane order and poly pathQuad and SequenceThese controls would move among lanes, but the part has one fixed source. The Cutups clock and region controls still work.
STAK and RATEBank BThe right pads play their own sixteen sample slots. BANK SAMPLE and BANK PADS replace the main engine controls.
RATESample and SlicesThese engines select a discrete lane at note-on and do not run a stack scanner.
VARISample or Slices in Quad or SequenceThere is only one source lane per part; variation cannot select another lane.
JOINSample or Slices in Forward, Reverse or Ping PongThe direct reader uses JOIN for its Loop seam only.
RATILanes or Sample with STR OffStretch Ratio is saved but does not affect audio until stretching is enabled.
Pads & Scale, Voice Engines, Stack PathSequenceThe main pads are trig steps; each sequence lane has a fixed audio source. SEQ SETUP handles note root, scale and pitch entry.
Prism DCAYPrism outside AccumulateOnly accumulation retains spectral energy after a frame.
Morph FREQMorph in ImprintImprint uses the second source as a shaping envelope.
Texture GRUPTexture in SpraySpray does not launch grouped events.
Stack Path SHAP, SKEW, CURVDraw pathAuthored points and their curves replace the named-path shape controls.

POS, FLD and SPD still affect the chosen source in Sequence; DUR controls the trig lifetime. A sample can end at the FIELD boundary before DUR. Sample STR/RATI can lengthen or shorten its transport, but does not automatically match DUR. For the individual engine controls, see Playback engines.
