Cutups

Cutups launches discrete source regions on a free or tempo clock. Region order, lane order, and polyphonic lane divergence are separate decisions, so several held voices can share a rhythm while following different material.

Good for: chopped loops, tempo-locked rearrangement, transient resequencing, repeated fragments, controlled random walks, and polyphonic lane counterpoint.

Lane Order choices

OrderFour loaded lanes
Down1, 2, 3, 4, then wrap.
Up4, 3, 2, 1, then wrap.
Palindrome1, 2, 3, 4, 3, 2, then repeat.
RandomA newly randomized loaded lane for each cut.
PairsTraverses 1, 2 as the first pair, then 3, 4 as the second pair.
Outside In1, 4, 2, 3.
Center Out2, 3, 1, 4.

Tempo source: Tempo mode uses BPM and Swing from Performance Loop. Pad pitch changes the cut reader, not Division, Free Rate, or the interval clock.
