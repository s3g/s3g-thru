Motion

Motion moves a read head through the Position/Field window. Continuous, Packets, and Motor articulate that trajectory; five segment sounds turn it into a bounded event instrument.

Good for: smooth source traversal, moving loops, evolving seeded paths, packet and motor rhythms, freeze/iterate gestures, layered pulses, and accelerating bounce events.

Pages and navigation

On Engine, set Algorithm to Motion. Press Up for the shared Pads & Scale and Amp Envelope pages, then Stack Path, Motion Path, and Motion Sound. Choosing Packets, Motor, or a segment sound adds only the pages that sound needs. Knobs 1–4 are the top row and knobs 5–8 are the bottom row.

The controls common to every playback engine are documented in the shared Engine page table. The sections below cover Motion-specific controls.

Mode choices

ModeTrajectory
ForwardMoves from the left Field boundary to the right, crossfading the wrap according to Join.
ReverseMoves from right to left with the same wrap treatment.
HoverAn eased out-and-back path with long, smooth turns at both Field edges.
Round TripA nearly constant-speed forward/backward traversal with short eased reversals.
Moving LoopRuns a forward loop, then advances the whole Field window after each completed cycle.
MirrorUses the Round Trip trajectory and reverses signal polarity on the return half, with Join smoothing around polarity boundaries.
ZigzagMoves through three stable time sections—forward, backward, forward—whose targets evolve from Seed, Travel, and Jitter, then continues with a new cycle. Stable section boundaries keep live Travel and Jitter edits continuous.
DrunkInterpolates through an evolving seeded chain of random targets. The end of one cycle becomes the start of the next.

Segment sounds

SoundEvent behavior
BounceRepeats around Position while segment width and level fall across the repeat cycle.
FreezeRelaunches the Field around Position without advancing the event center.
IterateMoves the event center by Event Step after each launch.
PulserChooses a random center in Field and derives a short segment from Duty and Event Rate.
DoubletsRepeats a center for the selected repeat count before advancing it by Event Step.
