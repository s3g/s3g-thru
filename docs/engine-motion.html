Playback engine 1 of 6

Motion

Motion moves a continuous read head through the Position/Field window. Path chooses where it travels; Sound applies a second articulation without replacing or reversing that path.

Good for: smooth source traversal, moving loops, eased reversals, deterministic wandering, tremolo-like packets, motor rhythms, and bouncing amplitude gestures.

Pages and navigation

On Engine, set Algorithm to Motion. Press Up for the shared Stack Path page, then Motion Path and Motion Sound. Knobs 1–4 are the top row and knobs 5–8 are the bottom row.

Engine page behavior

KnobParameterRangeMotion behavior
1AlgorithmMotionSelects this engine and exposes Stack Path, Motion Path, and Motion Sound.
2Position0.00–1.00Centers the travel window. Moving Loop treats a manual Position change as the next moving-window center.
3Field0.02–1.00Sets the width available to the path. The window is clamped at the ends of the sample.
4Speed0.05–4.00×Sets path transport speed and is multiplied by the played note's interval from Root.
5Stack Start1.00–4.00Seeds each new voice's independent phase in the shared stack path; on a Manual path it is the exact lane/crossfade.
6Scan Rate0.125–16 HzMoves each voice through the lane stack. Loop holds the current per-voice blend without stopping Motion's read head.
7Join0.00–1.00Scales read-wrap and reversal smoothing. On Packets and Motor it also controls gating depth: 0 leaves the sound continuous; 1 allows full closure.
8Level0.00–2.00Sets final output gain after the path and Sound articulation.

Motion Path — every parameter

KnobDisplayRangeDetail
1MODE8 choicesSelects Forward, Reverse, Hover, Round Trip, Moving Loop, Mirror, Zigzag, or Drunk. See the mode table below.
2TRVL0.00–1.00Drunk only: contracts or expands its deterministic random targets around the center of Field. 0 stays centered; 1 uses the full target range.
3JITR0.00–1.00Drunk only: offsets the timing within each interpolation segment. It is deterministic, so saved clips recall the same movement.
4–8Reserved—Inactive positions available for future Motion Path controls.

Mode choices

ModeTrajectory
ForwardMoves from the left Field boundary to the right, crossfading the wrap according to Join.
ReverseMoves from right to left with the same wrap treatment.
HoverAn eased out-and-back path with long, smooth turns at both Field edges.
Round TripA nearly constant-speed forward/backward traversal with short eased reversals.
Moving LoopRuns a forward loop, then advances the whole Field window after each completed cycle.
MirrorUses the Round Trip trajectory and reverses signal polarity on the return half, with Join smoothing around polarity boundaries.
ZigzagRuns three eased segments: forward to 72%, backward to 28%, then forward to the far edge.
DrunkInterpolates across eight deterministic random targets; Travel sets their span and Jitter perturbs segment timing.

Motion Sound — every parameter

KnobDisplayRangeDetail
1SND4 choicesContinuous leaves level open. Packets gates at Gesture Rate. Motor places four packet pulses inside an outer motor cycle. Bounce cycles through six accelerating, decaying bounces.
2RATE0.25–20 HzGesture Rate: packet repetition for Packets, the inner pulse basis for Motor, and the starting cadence for Bounce. Continuous ignores it.
3DUTY2–98%Open portion of each packet gate. It affects Packets and Motor only.
4MRAT0.05–20 HzRate of Motor's outer amplitude cycle. It is independent of the four-times-faster inner packet clock.
5MSYM5–95%Moves the peak of the Motor cycle, balancing its rise time against its fall time.
6MSHPTriangle / Rounded / PulseShapes Motor's outer cycle: linear slopes, smoothstep curves, or a two-state pulse.
7–8Reserved—Inactive positions available for future Motion Sound controls. Join and Level remain on Engine.

Path and Sound remain independent. Mirror and Zigzag still move backward where specified when Packets, Motor, or Bounce is active; the articulation changes amplitude, not transport direction.
