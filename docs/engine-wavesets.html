Wavesets

Wavesets divides a recording at rising zero crossings and plays those measured cycles in small groups. Repetition turns a fragment of recorded sound into a stable pitched tone; stride, order, source advance, and real-time processes can then pull that tone back toward the changing source.

Good for: extracting playable pitches from speech or noise, microscopic rhythmic loops, animated drones, crossing-level rearrangement, and transformations that retain the timing and contour of the recording.

Pages and navigation

Set Algorithm to Wavesets. Up opens the shared Pads & Scale and Amp Envelope pages, followed by Stack Path and Wavesets.

The controls common to every playback engine are documented in the shared Engine page table. On the waveform page, SPD combines with pad pitch: higher notes traverse each measured cycle faster. Position and Field choose the source area in which groups are found, while Join increases endpoint correction across each complete cycle. At Join 0 the recorded cycle is unchanged; at Join 100% a straight endpoint line is removed so both ends meet at zero.

Cycle safety

The engine follows the s3g-dsp Wavesets definition: cycles begin at rising zero crossings, crossing analysis can use the selected Detail filter, and crossings closer than four source frames are ignored. The left channel is the stable timing reference for stereo playback, so group timing does not change when the relative channel level changes. Playback itself uses the unfiltered recording and linear interpolation.

Wavesets queues live changes to Group, Repeat, Stride, Advance, Order, Process, Depth, Detail, and Join. The audible reader adopts the newest values at the next rendered zero crossing or low-level point; the following measured cycle boundary is the guaranteed fallback for DC-offset or heavily processed material. The old and new readers then share a short raised-cosine constant-overlap-add window, capped at 5 ms or half of the current cycle. Filtered crossings whose unfiltered stereo boundary would still make a large step receive the same transition automatically. Fractional oscillator phase is retained when the source advances, so a new group does not lose part of a sample period.

If the first bounded search cannot yet reach a complete crossing pair, Wavesets temporarily repeats an endpoint-corrected 20 ms unit while continuing to move through the active Field. Analysis retries from the new position, so a quiet lead-in cannot mute the engine or pin its cursor at the beginning. If a short Field contains fewer safe cycles than the requested Group size, the completed cycles form a smaller temporary group instead of leaving the control change pending forever. Crossing searches retain a fixed work limit and retry interval so silence and strongly DC-offset material cannot monopolize the audio callback.

Stack and polyphony

Every note owns its own crossing group, pitch, repeat counter, order state, and stack path. The current lane blend is captured when a new group begins, so moving between lanes changes the material without cutting through the middle of a measured cycle. Plain Loop freezes each voice at its current stack position; Hold on the Wavesets page freezes source-group progression instead. The two holds can be used independently.
