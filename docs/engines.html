The shared Engine page

KnobDisplayRangeWhat it controls
1ALG8 choicesAlgorithm selects Motion, Lanes, Grains, Cutups, Sample, Slices, Glitch, or Weave. Up/Down then shows the pages for that engine.
2POS0.00–1.00Position centers the active source window. Near either end, the window is clamped so it never extends beyond the sample.
3FLD0.02–1.00Field sets the active window width as a fraction of the sample. The waveform uses a short top tick for Position and a short bottom tick for Field.
4SPD0.05–4.00×Speed sets base source transport. In Grains and Weave it moves grain origins independently of keyboard pitch; the other engines combine it with note pitch as described in their guides.
5STAK1.00–4.00Stack Start sets an exact lane/crossfade for Manual stack motion, or the starting phase of a moving stack path. Sample and Slices round it to a discrete loaded lane.
6RATE0.125–16 HzScan Rate sets cycles per second for moving stack paths. Loop freezes the audible phase. Sample, Slices, and Cutups do not use continuous stack scanning.
7JOIN0.00–1.00Join scales seam smoothing for continuous/event readers. Motion also uses it as articulation depth. Cutups has a separate Cut Join for event-to-event fades.
8LEVL0.00–2.00Level sets instrument output gain before the safety limiter. At 1.00, the wet bus retains 6 dB of nominal headroom; 2.00 restores that gain. Pad velocity never attenuates playback.

One control, one location: a parameter appears on only one parameter page. Shared pages retain the same knob positions wherever they appear. A small hollow box marks an unassigned encoder position reserved for a future control; turning that encoder does nothing.

Shared Pads & Scale page

This page follows Engine for every playback algorithm. It chooses what the 32 pads do and, in Keys mode, whether they use the original chromatic layout or a folded scale layout.

KnobDisplayRangeWhat it controls
1PADSKeys / Slices / Cues / RollsChooses how the 32 pads address pitches, regions, stored positions, or clocked rolls.
2ROOTC1–C7Sets sample unison and the tonic of a named scale. Slices play this pitch; Rolls retain the pitches already present in their grouped capture. In a named Keys scale, the bottom-left pad begins one octave below Root before octave transposition.
3SCALChromatic + 24 scalesChromatic preserves the original semitone grid. A named scale removes out-of-scale pitches and maps adjacent columns to consecutive scale degrees.
4HOLDManual / 50 ms–60 sKeeps Shift+pad latches active until toggled, or schedules their release with one global delay.
5POLYOff / OnPolyphony Compensation smoothly controls summed energy as simultaneous source voices and Rolls increase. One voice remains unchanged. Turn it off only when intentional additive buildup is part of the sound.
6–8Reserved—Inactive positions available for future pad-layout controls.

Named scales retain an overlapping isomorphic shape: each row begins three scale degrees above the row below. Up/Down moves the complete layout by octaves. Scale affects the Move pads only; external MIDI and notes already stored in a performance clip are not quantized or rewritten.

Shared Amp Envelope page

Amp Envelope follows Pads & Scale for every playback algorithm. It shapes the amplitude of each of the eight source voices independently after that voice's playback engine. Pad velocity does not change amplitude.

KnobDisplayRangeWhat it controls
1ATK0 ms–10 sAttack time from silence or the current retrigger level to full amplitude.
2DEC0 ms–10 sDecay time from the attack peak to Sustain.
3SUS0–100%Sustain level held while the pad, latch, or recorded note remains on.
4REL0 ms–20 sRelease time from the current level to silence after note-off.
5–8Reserved—Inactive positions available for future envelope controls.

The time knobs use close-spaced millisecond values near zero and progressively wider steps for long fades. The default 3 ms attack, 0 ms decay, 100% sustain, and 25 ms release preserves the earlier immediate pad response while suppressing clicks. Envelope gestures recorded into a performance clip replay with that clip. Rolls use a captured, already-enveloped stereo fragment and retain their separate short edge crossfade; the Amp Envelope is not applied a second time.

Pad mode behavior

ModeWhat the pads doHold behavior
KeysPlay the chromatic or selected-scale pitch layout. With the Slices engine, pad identity also selects a region.Momentary; Shift + pad latches the voice.
SlicesSelect regions by pad identity at Root pitch. Use with the Slices engine for one-region-per-pad playback.Momentary; Shift + pad latches the region.
CuesPads 1–8 recall stored playhead positions. Shift + pad stores the displayed playhead; Delete + pad clears it.Storage moves to the safest stereo zero crossing within 10 ms on the selected lane. Recall starts a Root voice when none is active, or moves active voices through a short click-free transition.
RollsPad-down captures the combined stereo history of every active playhead, then stutters that fragment at 1/4, 1/8, 1/8T, 1/16, 1/16T, or 1/32. The six-division order repeats across the grid.Momentary; Shift + pad latches the grouped roll. Each simultaneous roll retains its own capture.

For a hands-on grouped roll, latch a chord in Keys, change PADS to Rolls, and press a division pad. The latched source voices remain active through the mode change and become the roll's combined capture.

Roll fragment length follows its division and the BPM at capture. The original playheads continue advancing silently while a roll is active, so releasing it returns to the live performance rather than a frozen source position. A 3 ms internal crossfade suppresses boundary clicks, and grouped rolls do not consume or steal any of the eight source voices. Performance recording stores the pad gesture and division; on replay, that event captures the active playhead group at the recorded moment.

Motion, Lanes, Grains, Glitch, and Weave all use this same page to shape movement through the four audio lanes. Stack Start and Scan Rate remain on Engine and are not repeated here.

2BLNDCrossfade / JumpCrossfade blends adjacent loaded lanes; Jump selects the nearer loaded lane.
3SHAPLinear / Smooth / Exponential / PlateauChanges the contour of the path before it becomes a lane position.
4OFST0.00–1.00Rotates the path through one complete cycle without changing Scan Rate.
5SKEW0.02–0.98Moves the path's halfway point earlier or later in the cycle.

What every engine shares

All engines can play up to eight voices from the four sources owned by the current performance clip. Each voice has its own pitch, read position, release, and physical pad assignment. Motion, Lanes, Grains, Glitch, and Weave also give every voice its own movement through the stack; Cutups, Sample, and Slices choose discrete lanes.

The later Mix / Rec, Effects, Sample Edit, Performance Loop, Overdub Takes, and Chain pages are available after every engine's contextual pages.
