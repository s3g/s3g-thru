Parallel effects · corner controls

Layers FX

Choose any of seventeen effects for each of the four Layers corners. Corners are independent stereo instances: two Reverbs, two Sprays or any other combination can have different settings and histories.

Hold a right-hand corner for about half a second, or double tap it, to toggle FX CORNER. Release leaves editing open. Repeat the gesture on the same corner or press Back to return. Alternatively, select CORN on LAYERS and navigate to FX CORNER. Knob 1 selects FX. Knobs 2–5 are the sound controls below. Knobs 6–8 set Z TO, ZAMT and LVL for that corner. Except for Dry, Filter and Pitch/Detune, a second FX DETAIL page provides four more controls on knobs 1–4. Its lower cells are reserved. Z TO destinations P5–P8 address these detail controls. See corner editing and pressure and release for those shared controls.

Lower the selected corner’s LVL when comparing strongly driven effects. Overall MIX blends the field with dry audio. Histories continue while their field weight is zero; changing an effect type clears that corner’s old history through a brief fade. Effects are prepared ahead of time, so type changes do not allocate audio buffers.

Choose an effect

On FX CORNER, knob 1 steps through the grouped order below. Click the main encoder to open the family picker. Turn or use Up/Down, click a family, then click an effect to apply it. Back returns to the family list; Back again returns to FX CORNER without applying. Left/Right changes family in the picker. Opening the picker leaves the current sound running.

Rerun, Shutter, Tumble and Spool offer free timing or SYNC to Thru’s BPM. Synced captures wait for a timing boundary. During clip playback, starts and loop boundaries reset the rhythm phase. Outside clip playback, synced effects share Thru’s running beat. Clips record the field, pressure and controls; temporary captured audio rebuilds from the instrument output when playback resumes.

Direct

Dry

The unprocessed stereo branch, aligned to the other corners. Use it as an anchor when moving between effects.

Starting point: Set one corner to Dry and use the other three for treatments. LVL on knob 8 trims the branch.

Tone / Color

Filter

A resonant stereo filter with a smooth blend between low-pass, band-pass and high-pass responses.

KnobDisplayRangeWhat it controls
2CUT25 Hz–18 kHzCutoff frequency; the upper limit follows the audio sample rate.
3RES0–100%Resonance around the cutoff. High values emphasize a narrow band.
4DRIV0–100%Oversampled saturation after the filter. Zero retains a clean path.
5RESPLP / BP / HPLow-pass, band-pass or high-pass response.

Starting point: Begin with RES at 15%, DRIV at zero and LP selected. Move CUT while blending this corner with Dry.

Tape

Tape coloration and transport motion adapted from the regular Tape effect. The corner has no added hiss, surface pops, crinkle or transport stop.

KnobDisplayRangeWhat it controls
2FMT2", 1", 1/2", 1/4", 1/8"Tape width in inches. Wider formats are cleaner; narrower formats reduce bandwidth and stereo width and add stronger coloration.
3REC0–100%Record level into the saturation curve.
4AGE0–100%Aging: darker bandwidth, head coloration, narrowing and gentle level drift.
5MECH0–100%Shared stereo wow, flutter and slow drift. Pitch and time movement increase together.

FX DETAIL

KnobDisplayRangeWhat it controls
1WOW0–100%Relative depth of the slow wow and drift within MECH. The midpoint preserves the normal depth.
2FLUT0–100%Relative flutter depth within MECH. The midpoint preserves the normal depth.
3TONE−100–100%Bandwidth adjustment around the format and age setting. Zero is neutral.
4WIDE0.0–2.0×Stereo side level after the tape format’s natural width. 1.0× is neutral.

Starting point: Start with 2", REC 20%, AGE 10% and MECH zero for subtle coloration. Raise MECH for audible transport motion. Duplicate Tape corners can use different formats and mechanical settings.

FX DETAIL

KnobDisplayRangeWhat it controls
1HEAD0–18 dBInput headroom: attenuation before the processor. Increase it when chords push the effect too hard.

Shred

A driven stereo circuit with feedback, input headroom, stereo-linked dynamics and filtered oversampling. The circuit selection changes the saturation and spectral response.

KnobDisplayRangeWhat it controls
2CIRC8 choicesSHRED, WOOL, RAT, ZONEA, ZONEB, FUZI, FUZII or DIODE.
3DRIV0–100%Coordinated input pressure and circuit drive.
4FDBK0–85%Governed feedback around the circuit. High values produce a more insistent, sustained sound.
5COLR0–100%Circuit tone and spectral balance.

FX DETAIL

KnobDisplayRangeWhat it controls
1HEAD0–18 dBInput headroom before the drive circuit. Higher values feed it a quieter signal.
2HPF20–600 HzInput high-pass cutoff; useful for keeping low notes from dominating a chord.
3DAMP1.2–18 kHzTwo-stage output low-pass cutoff.
4DYN0–100%Stereo-linked input compression and output containment for denser playing.

Starting point: Start with DIODE, DRIV 15%, FDBK zero and COLR 50%. Shred can be much louder than the incoming sound: reduce LVL before increasing DRIV or feedback.

Pitch / Motion

Pitch/Detune

Two overlapping sample readers transpose the incoming stereo sound. Fine tuning and a stereo offset also allow subtle doubling. Each corner owns its readers, so two Pitch corners can make different harmonies from the same source. Pitch zero, Fine zero and Spread zero preserve the original sound.

KnobDisplayRangeWhat it controls
2PITC−24–+24 stPitch shift in semitone steps, up to two octaves down or up. Fine tuning and Spread add to this value.
3FINE−100–+100 ctFine tuning in cents. One hundred cents equals one semitone.
4WIND20–180 msReader window. Shorter windows give quicker, more textured movement; longer windows give more sustained overlap and a softer attack. This reading time is additional to the common rack delay.
5SPRD0–100 ctTotal detuning difference between left and right. At 20 ct, the left is 10 cents lower and the right 10 cents higher than Pitch plus Fine.

Starting point: For doubling, use PITC 0 st, FINE 0 ct, WIND 80 ms and SPRD 12 ct, then blend with Dry. For harmony, try PITC +7 st with SPRD zero. This effect has one sound page; pressure destinations are P1–P4.

Rotary

A separate horn and drum rotate at related speeds. Amplitude movement, Doppler shifts and stereo placement give the source a rotating-speaker character. Speed changes accelerate or slow down over the selected ramp; RATE zero stops the motion at its current orientation. DEPT zero preserves the original stereo sound.

KnobDisplayRangeWhat it controls
2RATE0–8.00 HzHorn rotation speed. The drum follows at a slightly slower speed. The control gives more precision at slow rates.
3DEPT0–100%Depth of the Rotary treatment. Zero is the unprocessed signal; full uses the rotating horn/drum return.
4RAMP50 ms–8 sSpeed response time. The drum responds more slowly than the horn. Higher values give longer acceleration and braking.
5SPRD0.0–2.0×Stereo width of the rotating return. Zero collapses it to mono; 1.0× is its normal width.

FX DETAIL

KnobDisplayRangeWhat it controls
1XOVR120 Hz–5 kHzCrossover dividing the source between the lower drum and upper horn.
2BAL−100–+100%Horn/drum balance. Negative favors the drum; positive favors the horn; zero retains both.
3DOPL0–100%Doppler depth. Reduce it for amplitude and stereo movement with less pitch motion.
4TONE500 Hz–18 kHzLow-pass cutoff of the rotating return. Lower settings soften it.

Starting point: Try RATE 1.00 Hz, DEPT 65%, RAMP 800 ms and SPRD 1.0×. On FX DETAIL, begin with XOVR 800 Hz, BAL zero, DOPL 100% and TONE 18 kHz. A second Rotary corner can use a faster rate and a different balance.

Space

Delay

A stereo echo return. This branch contains delayed sound; Dry can be provided by another corner or the overall MIX setting.

KnobDisplayRangeWhat it controls
2TIME20 ms–8 sDelay time, with the maximum selected by RANG on FX DETAIL. Live edits crossfade between read positions.
3FDBK0–90%Repeat feedback.
4TONE0–100%Low-pass tone of the repeats, from dark to bright.
5PING0–100%Cross-channel feedback. Zero feeds each channel back to itself; full cross-feeds left and right.

FX DETAIL

KnobDisplayRangeWhat it controls
1RANG2 / 4 / 8 sMaximum TIME value. Select 8 s here, then set TIME on FX CORNER. The earlier two-second range remains the default.
2HPF20–1000 HzHigh-pass cutoff for new delay input; helps avoid a muddy low-frequency buildup.
3DRIV0–100%Soft saturation in the feedback path. Zero keeps this path clean.
4DUCK0–100%Lowers the delay while the source is active; repeats recover when the source settles.

Starting point: Try TIME around 250 ms, FDBK 35%, TONE 60% and PING 70%.

Delay Field

Four stereo taps build a wider rhythmic field. TIME and SPRD determine tap spacing; MOVE animates their placement.

KnobDisplayRangeWhat it controls
2TIME20–2000 msFirst tap time. RANG on FX DETAIL selects its maximum: 500 ms, 1 s or 2 s. At full spread, the last tap reaches four times TIME, up to eight seconds.
3SPRD0–100%Time spacing and static stereo spread between the four taps.
4FDBK0–85%Feedback through a softened, bounded return.
5MOVE0–100%Depth and speed of slow movement across the stereo field.

FX DETAIL

KnobDisplayRangeWhat it controls
1RANG0.5 / 1 / 2 sMaximum first-tap TIME. The last tap can reach four times this value: 2, 4 or 8 seconds.
2TONE1.2–18 kHzLow-pass cutoff in the feedback return.
3WIDE0.0–2.0×Stereo side level of the complete tap field. 1.0× is neutral.
4DUCK0–100%Ducks the tap field under active source audio.

Starting point: Start with TIME about 100 ms, SPRD 60%, FDBK 30% and MOVE 25%. Use a second Delay Field with longer times for an independent rhythm.

Reverb

The Plate and Room reverbs are available as independent parallel returns. Two corners can use different algorithms or decay times.

KnobDisplayRangeWhat it controls
2TYPEPLATE / ROOMPlate gives a dense, diffused tail; Room uses a compact feedback-delay network.
3DCAY0.2–20.0 sDecay time.
4DAMP500 Hz–18 kHzHigh-frequency damping cutoff. Lower settings give a darker tail.
5PRE0–200 msDelay before the reverberation begins.

FX DETAIL

KnobDisplayRangeWhat it controls
1DIFF0–100%Diffusion in the reverb network.
2MOD0–100%Movement of the delay lines inside the reverb.
3WIDE0–100%Stereo width of the wet return. Full preserves its normal width.
4HPF20–1000 HzInput high-pass cutoff before the reverb network.

Starting point: Try Plate, DCAY 2 s, DAMP 6 kHz and PRE 20 ms. Use the field to blend it with Dry. The corner is fully wet; field weights and overall MIX set the heard amount.

Spectral

Blur

Spectral smoothing spreads attacks into a sustained texture. Each Blur corner has its own spectral memory.

KnobDisplayRangeWhat it controls
2SMEA0–100%Spectral smear and the response time of the spectral envelope.
3PERS0–100%Persistence of the spectral tail, from a short trace to roughly eight seconds.
4TONE0–100%Spectral tilt. The midpoint is neutral; lower values darken the result.
5WIDE0.0–2.0×Stereo side level. Zero is mono; 1.0× preserves the normal side level.

FX DETAIL

KnobDisplayRangeWhat it controls
1SOFT0–100%Softens spectral envelopes and transitions between spectral states.
2DAMP1.2–18 kHzUpper-frequency damping. Lower it to make dense textures less brittle.
3TRNS0–100%Amount of original attack detail retained through the spectral texture.
4LINK0–100%Links part of the stereo phase movement while retaining independent channel magnitudes.

Starting point: Use SMEA 40%, PERS 55%, TONE 50% and WIDE 1.0× for a soft, sustained corner.

Spectral Spray

Move energy between frequency bins while retaining continuous spectral phases. Its memory can sustain and reshape a sound after the input ends.

KnobDisplayRangeWhat it controls
2SPRY0–100%Extent of spectral redistribution. Higher settings move energy farther from its original frequencies.
3DRFT0–100%Rate of continuous movement through the redistribution pattern. Zero holds the pattern still.
4HOLD0–100%; FRZSpectral persistence. The final position, FRZ, freezes the existing spectral magnitude memory; capture some sound before selecting it.
5DMG0–100%Spectral holes and phase drift.

FX DETAIL

KnobDisplayRangeWhat it controls
1SOFT0–100%Smooths fractional movement between frequency bins and changes in spectral magnitude.
2DAMP1.2–18 kHzUpper-frequency damping of the spectral body.
3TRNS0–100%Preserves original attack detail. Lower it for a more fully transformed texture.
4LINK0–100%Links part of the stereo phase movement.

Starting point: Try SPRY 45%, DRFT 25%, HOLD 60% and DMG 20%. For a frozen texture, let sound enter first, then turn HOLD to FRZ. Leaving this corner does not erase its memory.

Rhythm / Buffer

Buffer

Continuously collects recent stereo audio and repeats or rearranges short regions. This temporary effect memory is separate from Bank samples and saved recordings.

KnobDisplayRangeWhat it controls
2SIZE40–1000 msLength of the playback region.
3RATE0.25–4.00×Playback-rate multiplier. The centre is 1.00×; REPT also changes cycle traversal, so the resulting pitch and duration depend on both controls.
4REPT0–100%Repetition intensity. Changes cycle traversal, region variation and the duration of skip repeats. MOVE above zero introduces those skip repeats.
5MOVE0–100%Coordinated reverse probability, skipping and region-length variation.

FX DETAIL

KnobDisplayRangeWhat it controls
1XFD1–45%Overlap around region boundaries. Increase it for gentler seams.
2GLID10–250 msSmoothing of region size, rate and stereo geometry.
3GRIDFREE / 1/8 / 1/12 / 1/16 / 1/32Free or fixed skip-region lengths. These divisions use fixed free-running times; they do not follow the clip tempo.
4WIDE0–100%Spreads the left and right region lengths around the selected SIZE.

Starting point: Begin with SIZE 160 ms, RATE 1.00×, REPT 65% and MOVE 15%. Set RATE and SIZE differently on a second Buffer corner for interlocking repetitions.

Rerun

Capture recent stereo audio from the complete instrument output, including all active voices. HOLD freezes that material; LIVE refreshes it when a complete newer window is available. Until a complete window containing audio is ready, the live signal continues. Selecting it before playing does not freeze an empty buffer. Pitched repeats use filtering to reduce aliasing; high upward transpositions can soften the top end.

KnobDisplayRangeWhat it controls
2SIZE20–2000 ms / 1/128–1/1Reference repeat size. SYNC changes the display to note divisions. With HOLD, longer sizes can slow the available captured material.
3HOLDLIVE / HOLDLIVE refreshes the capture; HOLD retains it until you re-enter the corner or select LIVE.
4GATE0–100%Fraction of each reference cycle left open. Full preserves continuous repeats; shorter values add gaps.
5PITC−24–+24 stRerun playback speed and pitch. Higher pitch creates faster repetitions within the reference time.

FX DETAIL

KnobDisplayRangeWhat it controls
1SYNCOFF / ONTiming source. OFF uses the free rate or time; ON selects a note division at Thru’s BPM.
2SWNG0–60%Alternating long and short timing intervals for gates and capture refresh.
3REVROFF / ONReverse the held window.
4SEAM1–30 msCrossfade at sample seams and reader changes. The fade shortens automatically for very small regions.

Starting point: Use SYNC ON, SIZE 1/16, HOLD, GATE 100% and PITC 0 st. Add a second Rerun at 1/32 or +12 st. Moving between them combines different captured loops.

Shutter

Apply rhythmic amplitude envelopes to the ongoing sound. The source continues to advance while the gate closes. This effect does not freeze or repeat audio. Pulse edges and pan changes are softened automatically to reduce clicks, including during live editing.

KnobDisplayRangeWhat it controls
2RATE0.5–80 Hz / 1/128–1/1Envelope repetition rate, or a note division when SYNC is ON.
3GATE0–100%Open fraction of each envelope cycle.
4SHAP0–100%Blend from a pulse through a falling envelope, a sine-shaped envelope and a rising envelope.
5SWNG0–60%Alternating long and short envelope cycles.

FX DETAIL

KnobDisplayRangeWhat it controls
1SYNCOFF / ONTiming source. OFF uses the free rate or time; ON selects a note division at Thru’s BPM.
2PATTALL / ODD / PAIR / THREE / EUCL / SKIP / RAND / BURSTRhythm mask. RAND uses the saved seed; the other choices give repeating accent and omission patterns.

Tumble

Divide a recent stereo span into slices and play them in a different order. A complete newer span replaces the capture when available. The saved pattern and seed determine the choices. Slices retain their original pitch in either direction. SEAM controls overlap between slices without changing their playback speed.

KnobDisplayRangeWhat it controls
2SPAN80–2000 ms / 1/128–1/1Total source window before subdivision.
3PATTFWD / BACK / PING / SWAP / ROTA / SKIP / RAND / JUMPOrder of slices: forward, backward, ping-pong, adjacent swaps, rotation, skipping, seeded random or alternating halves.
4REPT0–15×Extra repetitions of each selected slice before the pattern advances.
5REVR0–100%Seeded chance of playing a selected slice backward.

FX DETAIL

KnobDisplayRangeWhat it controls
1SYNCOFF / ONTiming source. OFF uses the free rate or time; ON selects a note division at Thru’s BPM.
2SLCS4 / 8 / 16Number of slices in the captured span.
3SEED0–127Seed for random order and reverse choices. The same seed and clip timing reproduce the choices.
4SEAM1–30 msOverlap between outgoing and incoming slices. Real audio continues through each fade; the overlap shortens automatically for very small slices.

Spool

Slow or reverse captured audio, then accelerate back and return to the live source. At a stop, the return fades smoothly toward silence instead of holding a static sample value. Speed changes and re-entry are smoothed as the effect crosses between forward and reverse. Re-enter the corner, or change a control after a pass ends, to trigger another pass.

KnobDisplayRangeWhat it controls
2TIME50–4000 ms / 1/128–1/1Duration of deceleration. SYNC changes this to a note division.
3DPTH0–100%Strength of slowing and the buffered treatment. Zero leaves the live sound unchanged.
4REVR0–100%How far the slowdown travels into reverse. At full Depth, zero stops and full reaches normal speed backward.
5RCVR50–4000 msAcceleration back toward normal playback. This remains a free time when SYNC is ON.

FX DETAIL

KnobDisplayRangeWhat it controls
1SYNCOFF / ONTiming source. OFF uses the free rate or time; ON selects a note division at Thru’s BPM.
2CURVLIN / SMTH / EXPShape of the deceleration and recovery ramps.
3REPTOFF / ONLaunch another pass at a timing boundary after recovery, when enough recent audio is ready.
4SEAM1–30 msCrossfade at sample seams and reader changes. The fade shortens automatically for very small regions.

Pressure macros

On LAYERS, select Z CHAR. On a corner’s FX CORNER page, set Z TO to MACRO and raise ZAMT. Pressure now moves a small group of controls together. Positive ZAMT increases the treatment; negative ZAMT reverses that movement. The displayed sound controls remain the base settings. P1–P8 still select individual controls. Dry stays neutral.

FractureMore transformation, color and input conditioning.
ShredMore drive, feedback and color.

Performance and recording

All seventeen effects use the same pressure, glide, clip automation, corner copy/paste and project-recall controls. Clips and projects retain the algorithm and settings; temporary delay, buffer and spectral histories rebuild from incoming audio when playback resumes. Several simultaneous Blur or Spray corners and driven circuits increase processing cost. Check Shift + Menu during representative polyphonic playing on Move and reduce costly corner combinations if counters rise.

There are still fifteen playback engines. These seventeen choices process the output of the chosen engine in Layers mode.
