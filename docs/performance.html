Data layer

Performance loops

The recorder captures performance data—not a mixed audio bounce. Notes, hold time, pitch, the pad played, polyphonic pressure, stack position and Hold/Run changes, and knob gestures remain editable through engine settings and can be replayed at other rates.

Performance data flow

Capture

Replace creates a new base performance. Overdub adds an undoable take without changing the existing loop duration.

Storage

Each A01–D16 clip owns notes, gestures, timing, engine settings, and its four audio-source assignments.

Replay

The loop reconstructs independent voices; it does not play a stereo bounce of the original performance.

Record a performance

  1. Select a clip with one of the 16 Step keys. Use Left/Right for banks A–D.
  2. Choose REPLACE or OVERDUB on the Performance Loop page.
  3. Press Record. Notes already held are written at the beginning—or at the current loop position during Overdub—so they are not lost. If Loop is already holding the stack scan, each active voice's exact lane or between-lane crossfade is captured too.
  4. Play pads, change pressure, turn engine encoders, and press Loop to hold or resume stack motion. Parameter and per-pad pressure paths are captured continuously; stack position and Hold/Run are stored as stepped data.
  5. Press Record again. Replace closes at the exact duration and begins looping; Overdub merges the new pass without changing loop length.
  6. Press Play to stop or resume performance playback. Free play remains available and shares the eight-voice pool.

Recording is unquantized. Overlapping notes retain exact onset and duration, velocity, pitch, and which physical pad produced them. Continuous gesture curves interpolate across the loop seam, while recorded stack positions and Hold/Run transitions remain exact. Loop Rate scales note timing and gestures together from 0.25x to 4x.

Recorded versus live Stack Hold: a yellow Loop key and RECORDED STACK HOLD footer mean the playing clip is holding its stored stack positions. A bright-white Loop key means the physical button is applying a live hold. The live button remains an override; releasing it returns the clip to its recorded Hold/Run state.

Hold individual notes

Hold Shift and press a pad to latch that pad's note. Release both controls and the voice continues; its stable voice color remains on the pad. Use Shift + the same physical pad again to release it. Changing keyboard octave or Pad Mode does not release or remap an existing latch; this lets a latched Keys chord remain active when you enter Rolls. When an octave, Root, or Scale change makes that pad represent another pitch, the latch keeps the same dimmed voice color as an ownership marker. Return to its original mapping and it brightens again. Each pad is independent, up to the shared eight-voice limit. In Rolls mode the same gesture latches a grouped stereo roll without consuming a source voice; in Cues mode, Shift+pad keeps its cue-store function instead.

HOLD OFF on knob 4 of Pads & Scale is global. MAN is the default and leaves every latch active until it is toggled off. Choose 50 ms–60 s to auto-release latched notes. Changing the value reschedules all currently latched pads from that moment; returning to Manual cancels their pending automatic release. Each newly latched pad receives the current delay. Latched note-on and note-off times are captured normally by performance recording.

Replace, Overdub, and takes

Replace clears the old event data and establishes take 1 as a protected base. Every completed, non-empty Overdub pass adds a take containing its new notes and pressure/parameter gestures. An empty pass adds nothing.

Turn knob 2, UNDO TAKE, on the Overdub Takes page—or hold Delete and press Record—to remove only the newest overdub. Record Mode remains on Performance Loop, so knob 1 and knobs 3–8 show reserved boxes here. Repeat Undo Take to peel takes away in reverse order. The base take is never removed; use Replace or reset the clip for a new foundation.

Undo scopes differ: Delete+Record is take-only undo. Shift+Back is the one-level safety undo for a destructive clip reset, lane-source replacement, clear, or sample edit.

Clip banks

Each A01–D16 clip stores its four sample assignments, engine snapshot, duration, rate, timing mode, captured tempo, notes, gestures, and take membership. A clip holds up to 1,024 note events and 8,192 gesture points over a maximum 120 seconds.

GestureClip action
StepSelect immediately or queue the clip according to Launch.
Shift + StepCopy the current clip—including sources and all takes—to the destination.
Delete + StepReset the complete destination clip, including source ownership. Shift+Back can undo the last reset.
Left / RightMove between banks A–D from the waveform or parameter view.

Compose a chain

  1. Press Shift + Play twice from the waveform page to reach Chain.
  2. Press a Step key to select an entry.
  3. Turn the main encoder to choose clip A01–D16 and use Up/Down for 1–16 repeats.
  4. Click the main encoder to enable or disable chain playback.
  5. Hold Shift and click the main encoder to insert the active clip before the selected entry.
  6. Use Delete+Step to remove and compact an entry, or Shift+Step to move the selected entry.

The chain advances only at complete clip-loop boundaries and only after the next clip's four-lane source bank is ready. Selecting a clip manually disables Chain so a programmed transition cannot unexpectedly replace it.

On the Chain parameter page, knobs 1–5 control Chain, Slot, Clip, Repeats, and Clear. Knobs 6–8 are reserved; Algorithm, Join, and Level remain on Engine.

Capacity behavior

As a long or highly detailed recording approaches capacity, Thru removes nearly identical gesture points first. Playback continues at capacity, existing gestures retain their latest endpoint, and the footer reports CLIP FULL with note and gesture counts.
