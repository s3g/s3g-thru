Shortcut reference

The same control can change meaning by view, but recording gestures remain deliberately distinct: Record captures performance data; Shift+Record captures stereo audio.

Global and waveform controls

ControlAction
PadsPlay the current Keys, Slices, Cues, or Rolls layout. Keys and Slices use up to eight source voices; Rolls capture and stutter the combined stereo history of all active playheads at a pad-specific division without stealing those voices.
Pad pressureMove that voice's continuous stack offset when Pressure is Offset.
Shift + padLatch that pad's note or captured roll; repeat to release it. In Cues, stores the displayed playhead instead.
Knobs 1–8Edit the eight controls shown on the waveform or active parameter page.
Track 1–4Select the lane used by audio record, load, clear, and sample edit.
Main encoder turnSelect the previous/next lane.
Up / DownTranspose the chromatic or folded-scale pad grid by octave on the waveform page.
Left / RightSelect performance-clip bank A–D.
Step 1–16Select or queue that clip in the current bank.
Record or main clickStart/stop performance-data recording.
Shift + Record or Shift + main clickStart/stop stereo audio capture into the selected lane.
PlayStop/start performance-loop playback.
LoopHold/resume every voice's lane-stack scan at its exact crossfade. A released pad recalls its frozen position when retriggered until Run resumes. During performance recording, Hold/Run and each active voice's position are written into the clip. Sample playheads continue.
Shift + LoopIn Glitch, hold for temporary Buffer Lock; tap twice quickly to latch; press once while latched to return to live history.
MenuToggle waveform and parameter pages.
Shift + MenuToggle Help.
Shift + PlayCycle Main → Clip Overview → Chain → Main.
BackClose a secondary view; from Main, save and exit Overtake.

Editing and safety gestures

GestureAction
Shift + Track 1–4Open Move Samples and the Thru Recordings archive for that lane.
Shift + DeleteClear the selected audio lane.
Shift + BackUndo the most recent destructive clip, source, clear, or sample-edit action.
Delete + RecordRemove the newest Overdub take; the protected base remains.
Shift + StepCopy the current clip to the destination Step slot.
Delete + StepReset the destination clip and its four source assignments.
Shift + pad in CuesStore the displayed playhead in cue 1–8, snapped to the safest nearby stereo zero crossing on the selected lane.
Delete + pad in CuesClear cue 1–8.

View-specific controls

ViewControls
ParametersUp/Down changes page; knobs 1–8 edit aligned cells; Left/Right still changes clip bank.
Clip OverviewTrack 1–4 changes bank; Steps address clips; Shift+Step copies; Delete+Step resets.
ChainStep selects entry; main turn chooses clip; Up/Down sets repeats; main click toggles Chain; Shift+click inserts active clip; Delete+Step removes; Shift+Step moves.
Sample LibraryMain turn or Up/Down browses; main click enters/loads; Back goes up or closes.

LED meanings

SurfaceState
PadsIn a named Keys scale, playable pads are dark neutral grey and tonic pads are full white; the recalled map appears as soon as takeover opens. Active voices replace those colors with stable red, orange, yellow, green, azure, blue-violet, purple, and magenta slot colors. Momentary notes turn off on release. Latched notes remain lit until toggle-off or Hold Off expires; their voice color dims outside the stored pitch's pad mapping and brightens when that mapping returns.
StepsWhite occupied; orange active/stopped; green active/playing; red recording; blue-violet queued.
RecordRed during performance-data capture; orange during audio-lane capture; dim while idle.
PlayGreen while a performance loop runs; blue-violet while launch is queued.
LoopOrange while Glitch Buffer Lock is active; bright white for a live Stack Hold; yellow while the playing clip recalls a recorded Stack Hold; dim while scans run.
TracksSelected lane on ordinary pages; selected clip bank in Clip Overview.
