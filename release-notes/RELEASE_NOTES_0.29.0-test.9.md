# s3g Thru 0.29.0-test.9

Local testing candidate. Chain END now offers LOOP, STAY and STOP.

- STOP stops the performance transport at the end of the final entry's repeats, using normal note releases so effect tails can decay.
- The chain stays armed. Press Play to restart from its first entry.
- A final WAIT entry continues until NEXT is requested, then stops at its next loop boundary.
- LOOP and STAY retain their existing behavior. The new choice is saved independently for each chain.

Open Chain, press Menu, and turn knob 7 / END to STOP.
